# Open PDF and DOCX Viewers

Safe Appeals opens `.pdf` and `.docx` files in built-in viewers when you open
them from the Explorer (double-click or Enter). Spreadsheets (`.xlsx` / `.xls`)
open in the spreadsheet editor the same way.

Use Quick Open to jump to a file by name in the open case folder:

[Quick Open a File](command:workbench.action.quickOpen)

[Show Explorer](command:workbench.view.explorer)
