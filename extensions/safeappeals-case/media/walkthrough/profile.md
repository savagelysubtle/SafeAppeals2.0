# Your Profile Powers the Agent

Safe Appeals is built around an AI agent that helps you organize cases, draft
documents, and track deadlines. To do that well, it needs to know who you are.

**What we ask (once):**

- Your name
- Firm, union, or organization (or self-represented)
- Your role — lawyer, paralegal, claimant advocate, worker
- The area of law you practice
- Your default jurisdiction (e.g. BC WCB, Ontario WSIB)

**Where it goes:**

Your answers are saved on this computer only — in your settings and in a
personal agent rule (`~/.copilot/instructions/safeappeals-profile.instructions.md`)
that is automatically included whenever you talk to the agent, in any case.

You can rerun this step anytime with **Safe Appeals Case: Set Up Profile**.
